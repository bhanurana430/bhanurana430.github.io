Behind the work

There's a person behind all this.

I study at TH Deggendorf, work on research software, and tutor statistics. The interesting part, for me, is what happens between understanding an idea and making it work: the decisions, experiments, and occasional wrong turns that turn an equation or a model into something you can actually use.

Outside of that, there’s table tennis. Quite a lot of table tennis. I also like building small things just to answer a question-usually a question that seemed much simpler before I started.
